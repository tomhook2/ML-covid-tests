# ML-covid-tests
Predicting COVID-19 Risk to Improve Outcomes

## Context

Machine learning project for L7 AI and Data Science Hackathon.  
This word was inspired by this project https://github.com/nshomron/covidpred uploaded by user nshomrom

## Summary

Undetected COVID-19 can lead to:  
- Faster spreading of the disease with people not limiting their interactions with others  
- Increased pressure on the healthcare system. Bed capacity limit is reached quickly, planned operations are cancelled for example  
- Excess deaths are caused. As a viral infection, certain demographics are particularly vulnerable to severe illness or death, and so it’s critical we manage the risks

What is the problem?
- COVID-19 tests can take up to 3 days to return a result from the lab.
- For viral infections, the contagious peak starts 24 to 48 hrs before symptoms, and lasts some days, meaning by the time the test comes back, this peak would’ve likely already occurred
- Given this, can we predict whether the patient has COVID-19 at the time of the test taking place so the patient can be isolated, or test can be prioritised

Who can our recommendations benefit?  
- Government - they have a responsibility to look after the population.  
- Healthcare systems - demand for health care is peaking. Reducing the demand due to COVID-19 will leave capacity spare for other contexts  
- At-risk populations - if spread is reduced, at risk populations such as the elderly or those with particular health conditions will have reduced chance of a COVID-19 infection
